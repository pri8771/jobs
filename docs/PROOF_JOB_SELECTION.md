# Proof Job Selection Criteria

Artifact:
- A-PROOF-JOB-SELECTION

## Goal

Choose a real role that is worth applying to and is also suitable for proving the assisted/submission pipeline.

Technical convenience alone is not enough.

## Hard gates

A candidate proof job must be:
- currently live,
- a role the user would genuinely consider,
- compatible with known location/work-arrangement preferences,
- compatible with known work authorization,
- not below the hard compensation floor unless the user explicitly overrides,
- within a target role family,
- free of obvious mandatory qualifications the user does not have,
- reachable through an application destination whose policy/method is understood,
- not already applied to.

## Scored criteria

Suggested 100-point decision score:
- role/career fit: 25
- compensation fit: 15
- location/work arrangement fit: 15
- evidence/resume fit: 20
- company desirability: 10
- application destination suitability: 10
- posting freshness/confidence: 5

This score is a decision aid, not automatic authorization.

## Required selection record

For each candidate:
- company
- title
- requisition
- source URL
- canonical apply URL
- compensation
- location/work arrangement
- role family
- match score/reasons
- known gaps
- application destination/policy
- already-applied check
- packet readiness
- user decision: APPROVE / REJECT / HOLD
- decision timestamp

## Selection behavior

Present a short list rather than forcing a single technically convenient job.

The user chooses the proof job.
